The lore

Crab season never ends.

Most of the time the market goes sideways. Traders hate it. They call it a crab market.

A grid crab lives for it. It moves into your bag like a hermit crab moves into a shell, strings lines across the price, and pinches a little profit every time the price crosses one. Down through a line, it buys. Back up through the next, it sells.

Every win makes it grow. When it outgrows its bag, it molts into a bigger one and works more lines.

FAQ

Questions from the pit.

What is a grid bot?
A set of buy orders below the price and sell orders above it, evenly spread across a range. When the price drops through a line, that line buys. When it rises through the next line up, it sells. Each buy-then-sell is a round trip, and each round trip keeps the gap between the two lines as profit, minus fees.
When does a grid make money, and when doesn't it?
It earns in choppy, sideways markets, where the price keeps crossing lines. In a strong trend it can do worse than just holding: if the price falls below your range you end up holding the token, and if it rises above it you've sold everything and miss the rest of the move. The desk backtests your grid on real prices so you can see both cases.
Where do my orders live?
On Jupiter, Solana's main trading router, as limit orders owned by your wallet. Jupiter's keepers fill them when the price reaches your line. You can see and cancel them in the desk, or on Jupiter directly.
What does it cost?
Jupiter charges 0.1% per filled limit order on most pairs, plus normal Solana network fees. Each order needs to be worth at least $5. GRID adds nothing on top.
Does my crab re-place orders after a fill?
Your keys stay in your wallet, so every new order needs your signature. When a line fills, the desk shows the matching order one line over and lets you re-arm it in one click. That keeps the grid running without handing anyone your keys.
How does my crab molt?
It counts filled limit orders on your connected wallet, read from Jupiter: 2 fills makes a Scuttler, 6 a Pincher, 16 a Shellback, 40 a King Crab. Each stage unlocks more lines per grid, from 6 up to 50.
Is GRID part of Backpack or Jupiter?
No. GRID is an independent project. It works with the Backpack, Phantom and Solflare wallets and places orders through Jupiter's public API.
Is this financial advice?
No. Backtests replay the past, and the past doesn't promise anything. Crypto and tokenized stocks can drop fast. Only put in what you can afford to lose.
